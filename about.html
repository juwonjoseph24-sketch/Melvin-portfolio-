About Melvin Gadgets

Our story, mission and the values that drive us

Who We Are

Melvin Gadgets is a technology retail business founded with a simple goal: to make high-quality gadgets accessible to more people without complicated processes or inflated prices.

We started as a small store focused on smartphones and accessories. Today we offer a carefully curated range of phones, laptops, tablets, audio devices and everyday tech essentials — all selected for reliability and value.

Our team is passionate about technology and customer experience. We believe buying a gadget should feel straightforward, honest and supported from start to finish.

Mission, Vision & Objectives

What guides every decision we make

Our Mission

To deliver genuine technology products at fair prices while providing clear advice and dependable after-sales support to every customer.

Our Vision

To become a trusted name in personal technology — known for quality, transparency and excellent service across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Objectives

Offer carefully selected products, maintain strong supplier relationships, expand delivery coverage, and continuously improve the shopping experience.
